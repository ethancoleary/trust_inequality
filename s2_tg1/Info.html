Game 1

Pay attention to the following instructions as you will soon need to pass a comprehension check.

Game overview

The first game proceeds as follows:

  • The first mover decides whether to send ℙ10 to the second mover or send nothing.

  • If the first mover sends ℙ10:
    • This amount is tripled and the second mover receives ℙ30.
    • The second mover can send some, all, or none of this amount back to the first mover.
  • If the first mover sends nothing:
    • The second mover cannot send anything to the first mover.

Your decisions

In this game:

  • The first mover will decide whether to send ℙ10 or ℙ0 to the second mover.
  • The second mover will decide how much they would like to return to the first mover if they were to receive ℙ30 (i.e. the first mover were to send ℙ10).

First, you and your match will make your decisions as the first mover and afterwards as the second mover.

If game 1 is chosen to determine your bonus, then the roles will be randomly assigned to you and your match and you will receive your final bonus by enacting the decisions you both made in the assigned roles.
