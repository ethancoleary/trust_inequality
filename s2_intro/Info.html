During stage 2, you will interact with your match in two games. In each game, you will both make simultaneous decisions that may affect both of your bonuses. You will not learn about your match's decisions until the end of the study.

Your decisions and bonus

In each game, there are two roles (first mover and second mover). You will make a decision for each role (where applicable). At the end of this stage, one of the two games will be randomly chosen and one of the roles will be randomly chosen.

You will receive a bonus payment according to your final bonus from the randomly chosen game when you are in the randomly chosen role (and your match is in the other role).

Therefore, it is in your best interest to treat every decision as if you will be paid for it.
