During stage 2, you will interact with your match in two games. In each game, you will both make simultaneous decisions that may affect both of your bonuses. You will not learn about your match's decisions until the end of the study.

Your decisions and bonus

In each game, there are two roles (first mover and second mover). You will make a decision for each role (where applicable). At the end of this stage, one of the two games will be randomly chosen and a random number will be generated (1 or 2).

  • If the random number is 1, your final bonus payment will be determined by your decision as the first mover and your match's decision as the second mover in the chosen game.
  • If the random number is 2, your final bonus payment will be determined by your decision as the second mover and your match's decision as the first mover in the chosen game.

Therefore, it is in your best interest to treat every decision as if you will be paid for it.
