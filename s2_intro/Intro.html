For stage 2, you have been grouped with 209 other participants to form a society. You will be shortly matched with someone from your society.

Each participant in your society will be assigned to a group (A or B). This assignment will be made according to how their individual score in stage 1 compared to the scores of participants in a previous study.

A participant in your society will be assigned to group A if their score is at least as high as the average score in the previous study. Alternatively, they will be assigned to group B is their score is lower than the average score in the previous study.

The group assignment determines each participant's initial bonus for stage 2. Those assigned to group A will receive E£80 and those assigned to group B will receive E£40.

In this previous study, participants had 2 minutes to decrypt as many codes as they could. They faced the same sequence of codes and decryption keys as all participants in the current study.
