Stage 2 Intro

Pay attention to the following instructions as you will soon need to pass a comprehension check.

{{ if player.participant.merit == 1 }}

For stage 2, you have been grouped with 399 other participants to form a society.

Each participant in your society will be assigned to a group (A or B). This assignment will be made according to how their individual score in stage 1 compared to the average score in a previous study.

In this previous study, another group of participants had 2 minutes to decrypt as many codes as they could. They faced the same sequence of codes and decryption keys as all participants in the current study.

For each participant in your society, if their score is at least as high as the average score in the previous study, they will be assigned to group A. Otherwise they will be assigned to group B.

The group assignment determines each participant's initial bonus for stage 2. Those assigned to group A will receive ℙ80 and those assigned to group B will receive ℙ40.
