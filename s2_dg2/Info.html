Pay attention to the following instructions as you will soon need to pass a comprehension check.

The second game proceeds as follows:

  • The first mover decides whether to send ₰10 to the second mover or send nothing.
  • If the first mover sends ₰10, then this amount is tripled and the second mover receives ₰30.
  • The second mover does not make a decision.

You and your match will make a decision as the first mover. If game 2 is chosen to be payoff relevant, then the role of first mover will be randomly allocated to you or your match. Your final bonus payment will then be determined by the decision of the randomly chosen first mover (you or your match).
